# Studio law

Your rulings, collected from the spectre, phototroph and einstruct sessions of
August and September 2026, plus the failures they answer. Every check the
studio runs traces to a line here. Add a ruling by adding a line; never by
editing an old one (strike it and say why).

## Story

1. **Wonder before mechanism.** The first thing on screen is the phenomenon,
   not the setup. "Mechanism is what I want AFTER something amazes me."
2. **One viewer question, one number, one picture per episode.** The thesis is
   written before any render minute is spent.
3. **Explain from scratch, one concept at a time.** Every term is introduced
   once, with a picture, before it is used. The vocabulary chain is checked
   across the whole series, not per episode.
4. **The experiment record is not the story order.** Episodes follow the
   viewer's curiosity. A film that follows the commit log is a repo history.
5. **Repo-speak never reaches narration.** Banned: registered, card, P1, seed,
   exit 0, sigma, decimals beyond two significant figures, experiment ids.
6. **First person, past tense, the experimenter's voice.** Honest about
   mistakes, but a mistake is a beat, never a whole act.

## Picture

7. **No graphs, text or counters on the world.** Designed inserts are their
   own shots. Debug-looking overlays are allowed only as a deliberate style
   choice on top of honestly rendered materials.
8. **Real 3D scenes with the camera inside**, not microscopy from outside.
   Style B, astrophotography, is the current look.
9. **Truth in rendering.** Every particle is drawn where the tape put it.
   Simulation footage is never interpolated, upscaled or generated. Stock or
   generated footage is allowed only for real-world fillers, with provenance
   and disclosure.
10. **It must be clear what kind of matter is on screen in every frame.**
11. **No hard clipping boundaries.** Fade over one sigma; no particles popping
    through invisible walls.
12. **Per-particle colouring, greys by default, hue reserved for meaning.**
13. **Dark theme everywhere.** Never letterbox a light figure into a dark frame.
14. **Stills gate long renders.** Render options as PNG, get a ruling, then
    spend the lane. Styleframe before animatic before greenlight before render.
15. **Gates live at episode scale.** A contact sheet of every shot is reviewed
    as one image; six shots of the same grey disc fail even if each passes.

## Sound and pace

16. **Narration sets shot length.** Duration comes from measured TTS takes,
    never from word counts. At most five takes per line, ceiling ten.
17. **Not hasty.** A shot lasts as long as a viewer needs to read it; silence
    longer than a beat is a failing check per shot.
18. **Audio is part of done.** No master ships without narration, a music bed
    with ducking, titles and chapters. A silent excerpt is not a film.
19. **The voice is chosen by audition, not by a random draw.** One delivery
    voice for the channel.

## Capital

20. **Every GPU or render job goes through `exp run` with a priority and lane**,
    on the lane the manifest names. Renders are costed from measured coefficients
    before greenlight; a 58-day render plan is a rejected thesis, not a queue.
21. **API spend is metered per tree per round.** A loop that bills without a
    cap trips a breaker.
22. **Publish only on explicit approval.** Upload and exhibit are rulings.

## Review

23. **Three imagined viewers read every storyboard before a render**: a
    casual viewer, a graphics programmer, an editor. Their findings are kept.
24. **Any spoken number names the command and file that produced it**, and
    that gate runs before the take is recorded.
