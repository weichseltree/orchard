Your first visit

Come in.
Take your time.

Desktop, touch and headset share the same world. The button above is the only step between here and the first room.

Start on a laptop

Click the world to look around with your mouse. Walk with W A S D or the arrow keys. Press Space to pause a tape and Esc to return to the controls.

Explore by touch

Drag the world to look around. Use the stick at the bottom left to walk, and the playback controls to pause or move through time. Landscape gives you more room.

Look from a headset

Open this page in the Quest browser and choose Enter VR when it is offered. Headset and phone controls still need a full hardware check.

Before you enter

A visit loads the rooms and exhibits as you explore. They can use tens of megabytes, so Wi-Fi is a good choice. Weichselmind needs WebGL2; headset visits also need WebXR. Exhibits may be unavailable while work is underway.

What stays in your browser?

Your browser can keep copies of rooms and exhibits for later visits. Read about browser storage and privacy. The pictures on this page can be viewed without opening the 3D world.

Make room for your own curiosity

Try Weichselmind locally.

The local demo includes synthetic particles for exploring the controls. It needs Node.js and pnpm, and runs without research files, an account or cloud services.
