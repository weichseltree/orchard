Your first visit

Open a browser. Step inside.

Enter the grove →

On a laptop or desktop

Click the world to look around with your mouse. Walk with W A S D. Press Esc to release the mouse and use the controls.

On a phone

Drag across the world to look around. Use the stick at the bottom left to walk. Turn your phone sideways for more room.

In a headset

Open this page in the Quest browser, then choose Enter VR inside the grove when your browser offers it. Headset and phone controls still need a full hardware check.

What does a visit load?

The grove loads its rooms and simulation tapes as you explore. They can use tens of megabytes, so Wi-Fi is a good choice. Your browser can keep copies for later visits. Read about browser storage and privacy.

Can I look without entering?

Yes. The pictures on this page load without opening the 3D world. You can also visit the channel or read the source. The grove needs a browser with WebGL2; VR also needs WebXR.
