The short version

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising. If you walk into the grove, other people in the same room see the name you pick, where you stand and what you say. We keep as little as makes that work, and delete it on a schedule.

Your identity, without an account

When you enter the grove, your browser asks our token service (at /auth on this site) for a token. The service may first run Cloudflare's human check, Turnstile, which usually shows nothing. The token names a random identity for you. Your browser keeps it for 30 days and renews it, so you are the same visitor next time. Clearing this site's data in your browser makes you a new visitor.

The token service sees your IP address, as every website does. It does not store it. It turns your network (your IPv4 address, or the first half of an IPv6 address) into a scrambled code using a secret key that only the service holds. That code goes into the token. It lets the world notice that eight visitors came from one network, or keep a banned network out, without ever learning the address.

What your browser stores

We set no cookies. The grove keeps up to four entries in your browser's local storage: orchard.grove.pass (the token), orchard.grove.name (the name you picked), orchard.grove.guide-seen (whether you have dismissed the first-visit guide) and, from older visits, orchard.grove.token. The guide preference stays in your browser until you clear this site's data. Cloudflare may set cookies of its own that the human check needs.

Who else handles data

  • Cloudflare serves this site, the media and the token service, and runs the human check. Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Clockwork Labs hosts the world's database (SpacetimeDB). Names, rooms, positions, chat, reports and bans live there. Their privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask what is kept about you, and ask for it to be deleted, at any time, by e-mail to the address above. Because there are no accounts, tell us the name you used and roughly when you visited. You may also complain to a data protection authority: in Estonia, where the company sits, the Andmekaitse Inspektsioon; in Austria the Datenschutzbehörde.
